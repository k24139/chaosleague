Controls / Rules

Enter. Adapt. Win.

Solo: use WASD or arrows. Switch at any time. Two Player: P1 uses WASD, P2 uses arrows.

Move

A / D or ← / →

Jump + hit

W or ↑

Basketball: hold action near the ball to catch and charge; release to shoot at the opposing hoop. More charge adds power (full charge in one second). Move and jump as you catch. Rivals can knock it loose. In Soccer and Volleyball, tap action to kick or spike. Release before each new action. Jump next to your rival to launch them in Knockout.

BasketballPut the ball through the far hoop.
SoccerFind the far goal.
VolleyballLand the ball across the net.
KnockoutPush your rival into the void.

Each round brings one or two physics changes. First to three points wins the sport round. Three round wins takes the match. Points replay in the same arena. Escape pauses the game. Touch controls support multiple fingers.
